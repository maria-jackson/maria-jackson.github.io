I’m Maria Jackson. I’ve been a software developer on platform teams of nearly every flavor for over a decade. I like films, origami, industrial design, and going on walks anywhere in the world.

The essays on this site reflect my interests in the overlap of technology and systems thinking.

For the nerds: I’m interested in Platform as a Product, Product Thinking, Observability, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), Resilience Engineering, and DevOps, and how they work with AI.

For the super nerds: I frame it all through Dr. Michael C. Jackson’s [System of Systems Methodologies](https://systemsthinkingalliance.org/glossary/system-of-systems-methodology-sosm/).

Writers I enjoy include Marty Cagan, John Cutler, Camille Fournier, Will Larson, Charity Majors, Don Norman, Tanya Reilly, Matthew Skelton and Manuel Pais, the DORA researchers, and the folks around the [Resilience in Software Foundation](https://resilienceinsoftware.org/) and Lorin Hochstein’s [resilience engineering reading list](https://github.com/lorin/resilience-engineering).
